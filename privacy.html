Privacy Policy

Workspace Sync

Effective: September 9, 2026

Purpose

Physical AI Silicon Workspace Sync is a private utility operated by the domain owner to synchronize the owner's local workspace with the owner's Google Drive.

Google user data accessed

The utility may access Google Drive file contents and metadata needed to list, compare, upload, update and delete files as part of owner-requested synchronization operations.

Use and sharing

Google user data is used only for those synchronization operations. It is not used for advertising, behavioral profiling, marketing or resale, and is not sold or rented to third parties.

Credentials

This public website does not intentionally collect or store Google account passwords, MFA codes, OAuth refresh tokens or OAuth client secrets.

Retention and deletion

Files are retained or deleted according to the owner's synchronization instructions. The owner can revoke this utility's Google account access through Google Account security settings.
